-   **[Course Notes]{.underline}**

    -   PHIL 241: Logic

        -   Embedded tutors and instructional assistants

        -   Offer one-on-one tutoring, office hours, and group tutoring

        -   Managed via Writing and Learning Center

        -   Course offered spring and fall, if needed to be repeated

        -   NTR should take PHIL 241 in fall if they are not meeting the
            course requirement with transfer credit.

        -   FTF and COM should take PHIL 241 in their sophomore year
            after they have completed PHIL 230 and 231.

        -   Already built into Q2S flowcharts

    -   Ethics Bowl -- PHIL 385 (students enroll in 3 lab units,
        repeatable up to 12 units)

        -   Now only PHIL 385 (no longer PHIL 285)

        -   Students are recruited by Jacob Sparks -- Ethics bowl is
            usually a fall and possibly winter quarter commitment

        -   Take 385 in Fall. If they make it to Nationals, or if they
            wish to participate in Bioethics Bowl, they need to take it
            in Winter --DPR automatically recognizes the units. 3 units-
            students need to be aware that they may be a unit short.
            PHIL 400-1 unit to get 4 units, students need to check in
            with Ken about this possibility

            -   Will be fall + spring on semesters

        -   Send 1 or 2 teams of 5 up to Nationals/Regionals. Usually no
            more than 10 students have enrolled in the course, so all
            students have been able to participate in the competition.
            If more interest, may need to look at who gets to debate.

        -   Students look at 12 ethical topics -- 2 or 3 are debated
            upon at the competition

        -   IRA (instructionally related activities) -- covers students'
            expenses for competition (travel and lodging)

        -   22-26: removed 285 entirely and have 385 be repeatable,
            model curriculum after debate lab
